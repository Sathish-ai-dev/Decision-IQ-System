# AI Guidelines

## Objectives
Build trustworthy, explainable AI.

## Future Components
- Gemini
- RAG
- Prompt Manager
- Embeddings
- Vector Search
- Decision Engine

## Principles
- Human oversight
- Explainability
- Source citations
- Prompt versioning
- Bias monitoring
- Safety guardrails

## Out of Scope
- No AI in foundation milestones.
- Introduce AI only after datasets and analytics are complete.
